# Foundations for quantum states and calibration

Begin with the [topic diagnostic](../topics/mathematics-and-quantum-foundations/README.md), then follow the machine-readable `learning_path` order. The sequence has two roots: complex-number/linear-algebra preparation and classical probability. They merge at quantum measurement.

## Checkpoints

1. Convert between a complex number's components, magnitude, and phase.
2. Explain coordinates as basis-dependent descriptions of one vector.
3. Apply a matrix to a vector and identify an eigenvector in a small example.
4. Separate theoretical probability from a finite observed frequency.
5. Verify that a candidate quantum state is normalized.
6. Compute standard-basis probabilities from amplitudes.
7. Explain why a unitary gate preserves normalization.
8. Connect an oscillator's integer number state to Fock notation.
9. Order the evidence-producing steps from gate intent to promoted calibration.
