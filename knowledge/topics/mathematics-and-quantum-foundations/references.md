# Mathematics and quantum foundations references

## Claim map

| Pages | Primary support |
| --- | --- |
| Complex numbers and phase | OpenStax *Precalculus 2e*, complex numbers and complex plane |
| Vectors, bases, matrices, and eigenvectors | MIT OpenCourseWare 18.06SC sessions and problem sets |
| Probability and repeated trials | OpenStax *Introductory Statistics 2e* |
| State vectors, Born probabilities, and unitary gates | IBM Quantum Learning, *Basics of Quantum Information* |
| Operators, observables, dynamics, and oscillator number states | MIT OpenCourseWare 8.04 and 8.05 lecture-note collections |

Exact URLs and the shared access date are recorded in the [public technical source registry](../../research/public-technical-sources.md).

## Interpretation boundary

The knowledge pages give small derivations and examples to connect these sources. A derivation is labeled as explanation, not as a quotation. The pages remain `draft`: repository checks verify links, formulas, graph structure, and exercises, but they do not independently reproduce each provider's full course or assessment process.

## Exclusions

This topic does not attempt infinite-dimensional Hilbert-space rigor, spectral theory, wave-function differential equations, density matrices, generalized measurements, open-system dynamics, or device-specific pulse physics. Those need separate scope and sources.
