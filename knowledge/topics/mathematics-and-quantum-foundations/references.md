## Interpretation boundary

The knowledge pages give small derivations and examples to connect these sources. A derivation is labeled as explanation, not as a quotation. The pages remain `draft`: repository checks verify links, formulas, graph structure, and exercises, but they do not independently reproduce each provider's full course or assessment process.

## Exclusions

This topic does not attempt infinite-dimensional Hilbert-space rigor, spectral theory, wave-function differential equations, density matrices, generalized measurements, open-system dynamics, or device-specific pulse physics. Those need separate scope and sources.
