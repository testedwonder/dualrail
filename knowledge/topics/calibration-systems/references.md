## Authority boundary

- Public papers can establish system-specific controls and calibration procedures; they do not establish a universal production schema.
- Calibration records, validity windows, dependency graphs, promotion, and rollback are explicit explanatory software models unless a page cites a narrower public implementation.
- The synthetic dependency graph is locally executable educational code. It is not hardware data or a representation of any private architecture.
